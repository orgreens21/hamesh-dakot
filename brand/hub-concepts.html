ב. סטוריז: משחק אחד בכל פעם

Built for the phone habit people already have. The hub is a full-screen deck you swipe through like Instagram stories; progress bars at the top fill as you finish today's games, and it auto-advances to the next unplayed one. Turns "seven games" into one five-minute ritual. Risk: less overview at a glance.

ג. מפת הארץ

The most "ours" option. The hub is a playful illustrated map of Israel you scroll down from north to south; each game is a place on it, and a dotted trail joins today's stops. Finished games turn yellow. Nobody else can copy this one, and it explains "Israeli games" without a word. Risk: more illustration work to make it look great.
